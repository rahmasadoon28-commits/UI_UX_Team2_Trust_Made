## Project Overview

**Trust Made** is a digital platform that connects customers with verified craftsmen for custom furniture projects.

Today, customers often have to search for craftsmen through social media, ask for prices through separate chats, explain their requirements multiple times, and rely on photos or promises to judge the quality of the work. They may also have limited visibility into materials, project progress, payments, and whether the final product matches what was agreed upon.

Trust Made brings the whole process into one organized platform. Customers can submit a detailed furniture request, discover suitable craftsmen through smart matching, compare proposals, review verified portfolios, agree on project details, track progress, manage milestone-based payments, and complete a quality check before delivery.

The platform also keeps a **Furniture Passport** for every completed piece, creating a digital record of its specifications, materials, quality check, warranty, and other important details. After delivery, customers can continue using the platform for repair, repainting, reupholstery, renovation, or resale.

For craftsmen, Trust Made provides a professional profile, portfolio, verification, project opportunities, job opportunities, and access to learning and certification through the **Craftsman Academy**.

## Project Objectives

- Connect customers with suitable and verified craftsmen for custom furniture projects.
- Make the process of requesting, comparing, agreeing on, and managing custom furniture projects easier and more transparent.
- Build trust through verified portfolios, clear project agreements, progress tracking, quality checks, and documented project information.
- Protect both customers and craftsmen through milestone-based payments and a clear record of project details.
- Create a long-term digital record for each furniture piece through the **Furniture Passport**.
- Support craftsmen with professional profiles, job opportunities, training, and certification.

## Project Scope

- **Furniture Requests:** Customers can submit their furniture requirements, including dimensions, location, budget, preferred materials, style, deadline, and inspiration images.
- **Craftsman Matching:** The platform helps customers find craftsmen based on specialization, experience, ratings, and expected price.
- **Verified Portfolio:** Craftsmen can showcase previous work with project details such as materials, dimensions, estimated price, duration, implementation photos, quality checks, and customer ratings.
- **Proposal Comparison:** Customers can receive and compare offers from multiple craftsmen based on price, duration, experience, materials, ratings, and portfolio.
- **Project Agreement:** The final design, dimensions, materials, color, price, timeline, delivery date, and warranty are documented on the platform.
- **Project Tracking:** Customers can follow the project through stages such as Design Approved, Materials Selected, Cutting, Assembly, Painting/Finishing, Quality Check, and Delivery.
- **Progress Updates:** Craftsmen can upload photos and updates during production to keep customers informed.
- **Milestone-Based Payments:** Payments are divided into stages so customers do not have to pay the full amount before the work progresses.
- **Quality Check:** The final product can be checked against the agreed design, dimensions, materials, color, finishing, and visible quality requirements.
- **Furniture Passport:** Each completed furniture piece receives a digital record containing its product information, maker, date, materials, dimensions, finish, quality status, and warranty.
- **Post-Delivery Services:** Customers can continue to use the platform for repair, repainting, reupholstery, renovation, and resale.
- **Craftsman Profiles:** Craftsmen can manage their information, specialization, experience, portfolio, ratings, projects, and verification status.
- **Jobs and Learning:** Craftsmen can find full-time, part-time, and apprenticeship opportunities and access training through the Craftsman Academy.
- **Admin Dashboard:** Administrators can manage users, craftsmen, projects, payments, reviews, portfolio verification, quality checks, disputes, and fraud-related cases.

> **Final Deliverable:** A complete digital platform concept for managing custom furniture projects, including customer and craftsman experiences, project tracking, proposal comparison, verified portfolios, milestone-based payments, quality checks, furniture records, post-delivery services, and administrative management.

## Project Plan (5 Weeks)

### Week 1 — Research & Project Foundation
- Define the main problem and target users.
- Analyze the current custom-furniture journey and its main challenges.
- Define the customer, craftsman, and admin roles.
- Organize the main platform features and user needs.
- Define the core project structure and user journeys.

### Week 2 — User Requests & Craftsman Discovery
- Design the furniture request flow.
- Define the information customers need to provide, including dimensions, budget, materials, style, deadline, and inspiration.
- Design the craftsman profile and verified portfolio experience.
- Build the craftsman matching and discovery flow.
- Design the proposal comparison experience.

### Week 3 — Project Management & Transparency
- Design the agreement and project confirmation flow.
- Build the project timeline and tracking stages.
- Design progress updates and implementation photo uploads.
- Define the milestone-based payment flow.
- Design the quality check process before delivery.

### Week 4 — Furniture Passport & Craftsman Ecosystem
- Design the Furniture Passport and digital furniture record.
- Define post-delivery services such as repair, repainting, reupholstery, renovation, and resale.
- Design the craftsman jobs and opportunities section.
- Define the Craftsman Academy and certification experience.
- Connect the customer and craftsman journeys into one consistent platform.

### Week 5 — Admin, Disputes & Final Integration
- Design the Admin Dashboard.
- Define management of users, craftsmen, projects, payments, reviews, verification, and quality checks.
- Design the dispute management flow using documented project data.
- Review the complete platform journey from request to post-delivery.
- Finalize the project structure and prepare the complete deliverable.
